# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers, security engineers, engineering peers, conference communities, and potential collaborators who want to understand Pankaj Mouriya's writing and professional work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and technical publication where writing is the main attraction and the portfolio provides source-backed experience, projects, talks, résumé, and personal context.

## Positioning

The site connects hands-on security engineering with clear technical explanation through articles, illustrated guides, public projects, and conference talks.

## Operating Context

Visitors discover writing, read long-form technical articles and guides, review professional experience and public work, and follow GitHub, LinkedIn, résumé, project, and talk links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript, Fumadocs MDX, and source-owned Shadcn components.
- The About Me page uses the author's supplied portrait and source-backed biography. It must not invent pricing, client metrics, testimonials, availability for hire, or proficiency scores.
- Motion must pause or simplify for reduced-motion users, and interactive controls must remain keyboard accessible.
- Writing, portfolio, books, résumé, search, theme controls, and public profile links remain available through shared navigation.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Pankaj Mouriya, the established warm neutral, green, and terracotta palette, editorial display typography, readable body typography, and restrained technical motion. Personal imagery belongs on the About Me page rather than the homepage.

## Evidence on Hand

- Author-supplied portrait: `public/images/profile/pankaj-portrait.png`.
- Experience and recent talks: `src/lib/portfolio.ts`, sourced from the supplied September 2026 résumé.
- Public profiles: GitHub `pankajmouriya`, LinkedIn `pankajmouriya`, and `tsd.report`.
- Seven published articles, two illustrated guides, five résumé-backed roles, and nine listed talks are represented in the repository.
- No testimonials, client roster, pricing, or freelance performance metrics have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Put writing and technical clarity ahead of decoration.
- Use evidence already present in the repository.
- Let personality appear through composition, illustration, and precise interaction.
- Keep motion purposeful, finite, and accessible.
- Make every route useful on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, text alternatives for meaningful images, reduced-motion behavior, and responsive layouts without horizontal document overflow.
