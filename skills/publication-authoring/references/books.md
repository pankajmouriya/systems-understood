# Ebook workflow
## Existing ebook chapter

Create `content/books/<book-slug>/<chapter-slug>.mdx` from `../assets/book-chapter-template.mdx`.

Required frontmatter:

- `title`: nonempty chapter title.
- `description`: concise chapter summary.
- `slug`: unique lowercase kebab case within the book.
- `book`: exact registered book slug.
- `order`: unique positive integer that places the chapter in reading order.
- `draft`: boolean, defaulting to `true`.

Use a book-scoped public directory such as `public/images/books/<book-slug>/`. Resolve source images relative to the supplied draft before copying them.

The dynamic reader automatically provides the book route, chapter route, chapter rail, mobile menu, previous and next navigation, table of contents, search, and sitemap entries.

## Entirely new ebook

In addition to its chapter directory:

1. Add the new slug to the `book` values in the `bookChapters` schema in `src/lib/source.ts`.
2. Add one object to the exported `books` registry with `slug`, `title`, `category`, `description`, `audience`, and `source`.
3. Ensure at least one nondraft chapter exists before presenting the book as published. Keep the book unpublished when its reading path is incomplete or intentionally staged.
4. Confirm chapter orders are unique and form the intended reading path. Do not renumber an existing published book without showing the author the changed order impact.

Do not add a dedicated page component. `/books`, `/books/<book-slug>`, and chapter routes are generated by the existing readers.

## Editorial boundaries

Preserve the supplied edition history and source description. If the draft is migrated or historical, retain its original context without presenting obsolete instructions as current. PDF and EPUB export are separate tasks and are not implied by creating the web ebook.
